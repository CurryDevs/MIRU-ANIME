## 💜 About Miru

Miru is a sleek anime streaming app designed for Android. Watch thousands of anime with a beautiful dark interface, download episodes for offline viewing, and keep track of what you're watching with personal lists. Customize everything from theme colors to app icons. Your anime, your rules.

<br/>

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎬 Discover & Watch

- **Browse** trending, popular, and currently airing anime
- **Search** with autocomplete suggestions
- **Filter** by genre, season, year, type, and rating  
- **Watch** with a custom video player
- **Control playback speed** (0.5x to 2.0x)
- **Choose video quality** (auto or manual)
- **Skip intro/outro** with automatic detection
- **Toggle sub/dub** audio
- **Customize subtitles** (font, size, color, background)
- **Auto-play next episode** seamlessly
- **Cast to TV** with Chromecast support
- **Landscape mode** with episode menu sidebar

<br/>

### 📥 Offline Downloads

- **Download episodes** for offline viewing
- **Choose quality** before downloading
- **Track progress** with download manager
- **Resume** interrupted downloads
- **Watch offline** with full subtitle support

<br/>

### 📚 Track Your Anime

- **5-status tracking**: Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to Watch
- **Watchlist** to save anime for later
- **Favorites** to mark your top picks
- **Continue Watching** with exact progress
- **Watch History** of everything you've seen
- **Cloud sync** across devices

<br/>

### 🎨 Personalize Your Experience

- **8 accent colors**: Amethyst, Rose, Crimson, Emerald, Mint, Ocean, Gold, Sunset
- **Theme modes**: System, Light, Dark
- **AMOLED black mode** for OLED screens
- **Custom app icons** matching your color theme
- **Appearance settings** with live preview

<br/>

### 💬 Community

- **Comment** on episodes
- **Write reviews** and rate anime
- **See average ratings** from the community
